How to read the visualizer

  • Orange bars are being compared or examined.
  • Red bars were just swapped or overwritten.
  • Purple marks the pivot in Quick Sort.
  • Green bars are confirmed sorted.
  • Blue-cyan bars show the current working range for divide-and-conquer algorithms.
